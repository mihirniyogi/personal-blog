These few days have been a lot of ups and downs as well. Frankly, I'm a little tired. I'm a little tired of experiencing so much of an emotional rollercoaster. 

This morning, I was trying to focus on studying. It was working for a while before it all went to shit. Suddenly, I felt this hole in my heart. My chest sinking. It's like after spending 4 days with the group, always around people, suddenly I forgot how to be with myself. 

Moreover, my feelings for Nat are still there. To me, it's pretty logical that a relationship isn't going to or should happen between the two of us. I find that some of her qualities aren't my favourite. For example, she can be a little overbearing, or alpha/dominating. Which is fine; I don't judge. And I feel she's a little idealistic/immature. Or maybe she just acts that way. Notwithstanding those 2 qualities...ah heck I still got feelings for her. 

Every time she smiles. Or puts her arms on me. Also there's conflicting emotions. I'm a person who constantly needs validation due to my insecurities. So I constantly seek that out, or at least I'm really happy when I do get validated. Simple things like "wah, driving for so long must have been tiring". 

That need for validation _in general_, combined with my need for validation _from the person whom I have feelings for_, wasn't a great look on me during the trip, to be very honest. 

Now suddenly there's silence. I'm by myself, just studying. This morning, I couldn't take it and teared up a little, screamed into my hands a little. I realised it's high time I sought professional help. I reached out to the university's counselling service, booked myself an initial appointment in the afternoon, and rushed there as I was almost late. 

The counsellor, Esin, was a little lowkey, low-energy, detached. She listened intently though. It wasn't as "supportive" as I would have liked it to be. I did feel validated a little, since she said the usual "it must be tough having to deal with so many of these emotions at once". I told her basically a summarised version of everything that's transpired thus far. 

She quickly connected the dots and identified a pattern of me "shutting things down", especially with that whole Grace story. She further suspected the Grace story had a semblance of truth too, which frankly, maybe it did now that I'm thinking about it. She's very quick and good at putting the pieces together. 

That's when the time ran out, unfortunately, and the next appointment is weeks away (22nd). Right after the appointment, I felt...actually much better in that moment. I felt like I was making progress vis-a-vis my emotions. I felt good, so I travelled to the main campus, studied there for a while, invited my friends but they said they were too tired and would join tomorrow instead.

The nights are the worst, I think. At least writing this out helped clarify and verbalise some thoughts. I will probably cool down, meditate or do breathing exercises and sleep soon. I'm also scared to be "calm" again but whatever that's for another journal entry. hot beans.
