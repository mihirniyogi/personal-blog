Today I met Grace for lunch. I was for some reason a little unsure of how it was gonna go. Especially with how I was gonna carry myself, what kind of facade I would be portraying. But I was mostly myself. I liked how I portayed myself this time. I was cheerful but maybe not my most authentic in that I didn't say much. She had a LOT to say though - in fact, she carried like 90% of the convo. What surprised me was that she talked about something _other than the office_.

She told me about her China/Japan trip in detail (the piercings and ear surgery, her family, the racist police). I was actually beyond surprised and a little confused that she shared these with me but I of course am more than happy to listen to interesting stories. She tells them pretty well too. 

Of course the rest of the time was about how much of a difficult time she was facing in the office. The politics are apparently a lot worse and she's very obviously had her soul sucked out or something. She's so much less energetic than before despite finally graduating and you know being a free adult or whatever. I feel SO BAD I want to cry on her behalf. Bias or not I think it's pretty clear that she's being treated weirdly and unfairly. The office culture just isn't it :(

I felt bad that I didn't say much in support. I mean - I did but not much. But I am happy that I actively listened. Due to the circumstances I'm probably one of the few people to uniquely understand her situation which is probably why she feels comfortable sharing with me. 

This makes me re-evaluate the friendship though. I'm not sure how calculative this is or something. But ideally I would like my friendships to be a bit more than surface-level, and also two-way. Maybe next time we meet I should tell her more of my stories? But what stories? What could she possibly relate to? 

In general, what stories do I tell people? There's always definitely something that's going on in my mind; some theme or story. For example, this week the theme is adjusting back to Singapore life, and drawing all the comparisons in the world. So maybe that. It doesn't need to be exactly relatable I guess but more authentic and more myself so that people have an insight as to what's going on and I don't look like a thoughtless blob who's just smiling all the time.
