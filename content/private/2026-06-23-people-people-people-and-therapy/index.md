### part 1: people

For the past few days I've been spending a LOT of time with the people I love. 

The (last) movie night with the group🥰 on 19th, Duc on 20th, Brandon on 21st, Ben, the boxing group and Nat on 22nd, Nat and Duc, and then Angelica and Hope today, the 23rd. And possibly Duc tomorrow, on the 24th, for souvenir shopping. It's been a whirlwind. 

Of course, the last movie night was pretty emotional. I wanted to write a journal entry then but I think I physically could not. Letters were handwritten, photos were colour printed, charcuterie bowls were adorned. The movie was hilarious and we had so much fun. Everyone was comfortable with each other. However, I felt pretty devastated - I think because this was coming to an end. "This" being the special time we're spending together in this environment of relaxation and heightened enjoyment. Exchange! Of course, we'd see each other back in SG, and Hope said something today that stuck with me - it's not going to be over, but rather the start of something _different_, something new. 

Of course I wrote in another entry about how Duc and I geeked out over frontend dev. I also got to meet Brandon for the first time in 6 months. I'll see him again at the end of the year, of course. He was a little more...I think he had more conviction in his beliefs. He knew what he wanted and he wasn't ashamed or unsure of it. Whether it be the people he hangs out with, the intellectual interests he pursues, or the career paths he can take. He's forming stronger opinions which is a welcome sight to see in my best friend :)

I am sad I won't see him for some time, but I'm beyond happy that this surprise visit happened. Nat mentioned an Indonesian word "puas" which means content, satisfied. I think that's how I feel.   

On the 22nd, I met the boxing group for lunch, typical Singaporean humour things (east side best side, and they had a whole thing about parkway parade). I don't think I particularly connect wiith Roshin or Elysia but they're cool people nevertheless! Gabriel's a surprisingly sensitive to people's behaviours, so I think he engages with me a little more to loop me in to the conversation. Surprising, but much appreciated. Of course Ben was there. I don't think he particularly connected, but Gab and Ben both know Keanan apparently, so like worlds colliding🤯...we'll probably all meet in SG in december?  

I then met Ben again where we ate our respective dinners, talked about my counselling session and then studied for quite a while. I actually really enjoyed helping him understand the machine learning content, and it also upgraded my own understanding - standard TA things. I said my goodbyes at the end, hugged and saluted him away. 

I continued that night with Nat, where we yapped, studied and then yapped so much more LOL. Slept at 4.30am. We're both professional, olympic-grade yappers I must say. Vibes: immaculate. Sleep: nonexistent. Worth it? Absolutely. Upon me bringing up my counselling and small snippets from my past, she shared about her christian values and deliverance. It was lowkey impressive that she went through with it, had the courage to list out her sins and share with her family pastor and her family etc. Very cool, and it seemed to have a healing effect, almost. I'm curious because it sounds almost analogous to counselling/therapy. I'm touched because she was able to share that part of her life with me. 

The exam happened today, of course. It was ok, passable. Duc, Nat and me randomly roamed until we found this mix of food places in an RMIT building. We talked and again, vibes immaculate. I think both of them were a little sad that it's ending. They hugged at the end because it was probably the last time they would meet here. 

I walked back with Nat, we hugged goodbye just in case. She looked a little stressed, a little sad (?) 🥺. I really hope she feels better though once she meets her mates in Sydney.  

Eventually met Angelica and Hope for movie night! We watched Hercules! It was a great, classic, Americanised Disney movie with like jokes and adult innuendos. We talked a little bit after, scrolled through some movies on Disney. I hugged Hope goodbye, again just in case. God it's hard every time. But worth it.

I'm pretty sleepy so I'll update the part 2 tomorrow maybe.
