I felt quite sick after the cairns trip, a little sad that I said a (maybe) goodbye to hope. 

I felt (almost) ashamed to go to the comedy show alone.

But oh boy was the show everything I hoped it was and THEN some. Usually comedy shows I watch of my favourite comedians are like a little less perfect than their specials on youtube and they put in a little less energy than normal. 

It started out normal but every quickly turned into this super high-impact, high-energy performance. He was so relaxed and in his element, rarely breaking character. Even the crowd work was on point. 

There were a surprising number of white people in the audience - awesome that he's becoming more international. 

He made relatable references to indian culture / language. I've ignored that part of my life so long that it's almost no longer part of my identity. But this felt nice, almost reconnecting with my roots, my upbringing (?)

I also laughed my ass off during the show, letting loose...
