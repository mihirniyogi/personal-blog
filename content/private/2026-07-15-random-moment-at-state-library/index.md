I was sitting in front of the state library in melbourne, the beautiful violin busker having caught my ear. This man, presumably in his 60s, comes up to me and asks if I've heard of Jesus. When I say no (and I'm trying to be as respectful as possible to decline), he simply asks if he can pray for me. I say yes. He chants a simple well-wishing using my name. I felt he was genuinely just wishing me well. I felt love from this random stranger. He says he is Jonathan from Indonesia.

After he walks away, I turn my attention to the violinist who is playing a Lindsey Stirling version of Carol of the Bells and it is beautiful (not Lindsey good but she was good nonetheless). I have a little realisation. 

I've been spending so much time with people and not that much with myself. I think I needed a little nervous system reset too. So instead of trying to "get things done", I'll probably listen to what I need and do what I feel like.
