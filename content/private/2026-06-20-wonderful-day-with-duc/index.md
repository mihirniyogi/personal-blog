After Nat and Angelica's surprises yesterday, Duc and I were feeling particularly emotional and down. We studied together for a while. We both ate our respective cooked dinners and yapped all evening. 

At first the discussion was more reserved. I was trying to push it forward by asking weird or exciting questions. But the barriers (I guess that we both put up) started to come down. It's like we were on different frequencies for a while before we finally clicked on...Computer Science! Of course that's the common topic. But it wasn't about CS exactly; it was about web development and our shared nerdiness of frontend web design. It couldn't get more niche than this. 

It has been SO long since I nerded out with someone regarding web development. I think it started with me sharing about my self-hosted blog and personal website. He mentioned he's been trying to set up something like this, but this is the first time he's seen a self-hosted setup and now he's inspired.

_He_ then gets excited and starts sharing about his Duck website (personal project competitive programming multiplayer game). Of course I had seen it 2 nights ago but now when I really dived into it it's SO IMPRESSIVE. The frontend details are _beautiful_ - detail-oriented and frankly very well thought out. And we spend a solid hour and a half just going through the website, the code. We're shooting ideas off each other regarding potential concepts. I ask if he implemented a feature this way, and he _immediately_ understands my intention because this is such a...frontend thing. It's like only the two of us know about it. Ahhh it's just us nerding out so hard. 

Somehow the conversation shifts to layers and onions. He asks me if I ever cringed at something I've created. I was like ?? AND HE REVEALS that he has a 200-THOUSAND word novel posted somewhere online that's hard to find. This is what late night convos are about LOL. And he wrote a comic but lost it. 

Somewhere in the mess we talk about taking the most ridiculous mod GEC1032 (Home) and I READ HIS REVIEW ON NUSMODS AND DECIDED TO TAKE IT UP BECAUSE OF IT AND THAT'S WHY HIS NAME FELT AWFULLY FAMILIAR IN THE EXCHANGE GROUP CHAT AAAAAAAAAAA. And a lot about his life was revealed when he showed me his very personal essay that he somehow scored full marks for ?? About his conservative family in the past, his abusive guardian etc.   

I find myself just wondering in the middle of that moment - how did I get so close with _all_ the members in the group? Duc says it's circumstance, but it can't possibly be _just_ circumstance?

Anyway, the conversation wraps up and we both can feel ourselves being in much better spirits; moods lifted.

I'm not gonna investigate further or dig deep. I think I'll just accept these feelings of happiness for now. And continue to work on my attachment problems (I think it's going semi-well).
